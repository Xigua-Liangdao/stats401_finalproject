==================================================
RULES FROM LPL GUIDELINES
==================================================

- Headlines stay short, punchy, uppercase
- Do not over-track or over-lead
- Do not use the secondary face for headlines
- Do not use the headline face for long body copy
- Chinese headlines should match English size when possible;
  half-size is OK when the layout is copy-heavy
